Privacy policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

This policy covers the private tool used to run the simplyglutenfree.recipes Instagram account.

What the tool accesses

The tool signs in with the account owner's own Google account and accesses only the owner's own Google Drive and Google Sheets. It uses them to store recipe drafts and images, and to post that content to the owner's own Instagram account.

What it doesn't do

  • It is not offered to the public and nobody else signs in to it.
  • It doesn't collect, store or share any personal data about other people.
  • It doesn't sell, transfer or share any Google user data with third parties, and doesn't use it for advertising.

Google user data

The tool's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Access can be removed at any time from Google Account permissions.
