simplyglutenfree.recipes

simplyglutenfree.recipes is an Instagram account sharing one naturally gluten-free recipe a day, made from everyday ingredients, with a photo of the dish, the ingredient list, nutrition information per serving and step-by-step method cards. Recipes are prepared with the help of a small private tool that the account owner uses to organise drafts in their own Google Drive and Google Sheets and to post them to their own Instagram account.
